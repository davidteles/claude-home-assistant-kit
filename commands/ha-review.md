Review this Home Assistant instance and report, ranked by impact on the household:

1. **Safety and security first:** offline smoke/CO/leak sensors, alarm triggers, low batteries on safety devices, integrations needing re-authentication.
2. **Broken things people notice:** failing automations (read recent traces), unavailable entities that dashboards or voice assistants use, notifications that are cut off or never clear.
3. **Quick wins:** naming, missing categories/labels, duplicated logic, dashboards with dead cards.

Follow the `ha-house-rules` skill. Don't change anything in this pass. End with a short list the user can approve item by item (or offer a triage PDF with the `ha-triage-pdf` skill).
